### Shallow sewer cover review

1. Open the example and hover the first sentence. The actual main-cover provision opens on PDF page 11, printed page 5.
2. Select the service discrepancy to compare the two editions. Explain that Basis identifies the issue and the clarification needed; it does not invent a ruling about which document overrides the other.
3. Select the final sentence to see the obligation to communicate changed property-line elevations before EPCOR construction.

### Drainage capacity after expansion

1. Start with the query's inputs. The new 1.20 m³/s estimate is hypothetical project information, not a result calculated by this frontend.
2. Hover the capacity conclusion to compare the two threshold tables. Emphasize that 25% applies to the revised five-year runoff, not the old catchment flow or the pipe diameter.
3. Select the pond sentence: a method permitted for minor conveyance does not automatically qualify for storage design. Then show the separate major-conveyance basis.

### LID infiltration acceptance

1. Open the report and select the testing sentence. It opens PDF page 97 directly, despite the manual's earlier default opening page.
2. Show how the supplied 500 m² area and 600 mm soil depth select the applicable testing provisions and exclude the shallow-soil exemption.
3. Compare the growing-media and soil-cell subgrade passages. Similar-looking density values have different scopes.
4. Select the construction-protection sentence to bring in the other guides. All three document sources remain accessible through tabs and the comparison selector.

## Impact and real-world relevance

The central value is connecting a practical decision to verifiable evidence across documents: what needs revision, what needs calculation, and what remains unproven. Intended users include civil designers, project coordinators, construction inspectors, and asset owners.

These examples demonstrate potential value, not measured savings. To validate the judging claim, ask practicing engineers to review the scenarios, then compare manual review with Basis using the same documents and questions. Measure time to locate and verify critical provisions, missed requirements, incorrect interpretations, and whether the proposed next action is appropriate. Report observed results and participant feedback rather than assigning invented dollar savings or claiming that this prototype has prevented a real failure.
