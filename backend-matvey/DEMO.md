# Basis project demonstration

Double-click root **Start Basis.cmd**, then choose **5** to open Basis. Keep the controls open for crash recovery. Choose **4** and wait for READY with zero indexing failures. Restart/reindex is **2**, diagnostics **6**, logs **7**. Public access requires this computer, network and HTTPS tunnel online. No connection-key entry is needed.

Before presenting, open both projects and use **Reset demo**. This restores prepared history and open findings. The comparisons are explicitly labeled prepared, source-verified evidence and require no timed inference. Two projects and six synthetic project PDFs are permanent protected seeds.

## Two-minute sequence

1. **0:00–0:20** — Overview: Mill Creek Corner Infill and Willow Creek Bridge Renewal. Each has its own client, documents and technical scope.
2. **0:20–0:40** — Open Mill Creek. Show its client brief, concept drawing, site note and ten selected municipal/EPCOR references. Filter or open a document.
3. **0:40–1:10** — Reopen “Does the concept box-planter width agree with the infill guideline?” from history. The concept allocates 400 mm; the guideline states at least 450 mm. The report asks for dimension-convention confirmation and coordinated revision.
4. **1:10–1:30** — Select/hover the citation. Original guideline PDF page 18 and concept page 1 appear side by side with highlighted excerpts and minimap markers. Wait for PDF rendering.
5. **1:30–1:55** — Return via Project dashboard. Inspect the underdrain finding: concept connection to combined C1 versus the guideline page 20 prohibition. Dismiss it as reviewed. Dismissal persists after reload.
6. **1:55–2:00** — Close with organized project context, original-source verification and potential issues for professional review.

For a second-domain demonstration, open Willow Creek. Its six documents include fictional client, concept and calculation PDFs plus three actual Alberta references. Inspect 1% crossfall versus 2% away from the crown on a non-super-elevated deck. The second finding concerns 0.35% longitudinal grade requiring approval below 0.5%; it is not an automatic prohibition. The client explicitly adopts BSDC v9 January 2022, without asserting it is the latest edition.

## Recovery and limits

If public access fails, launcher **5** opens the local interface. Prepared reports need the local API but no model service. **H** establishes HTTPS; **P** explicitly publishes a changed hostname. Do not change inference profiles or repair dependencies during presentation. The previous demo runtime is retained separately during rollout.

Synthetic project documents are labeled demonstration material and not for construction. Findings are prepared review prompts, not automated compliance determinations. General uploads and general automatic contradiction detection are deferred. See PROJECT_API.md for integration and persistence details.
